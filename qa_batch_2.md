## Q1: [Securities Crime] What is insider trading crime and how to defend against it?

**A**: Insider trading crime refers to insiders or those illegally obtaining inside information trading securities before information is public. Defense points: 1) Whether information qualifies as statutory inside information; 2) Whether defendant is statutory insider; 3) Causal relationship between trading and inside information.

**法律依据**: 《证券法》第76条

**领域**: 证券犯罪 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Criminal Defense] For Expatriates: How to protect trademark rights against infringement?

**A**: Trademark infringement remedies: 1) Collect and preserve evidence including notarized purchases; 2) Send cease and desist letter; 3) File complaint with market supervision authority; 4) File lawsuit for injunction and damages; 5) Compensation based on actual losses, infringer profits, or royalty multiples.

**法律依据**: 《专利法》

**领域**: 知识产权 | **语言**: English
